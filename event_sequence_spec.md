# Clinical Pathway Event Sequence Specification

## Standard Patient Journey Sequence
1. **Triage / Registration**: Initial patient arrival and vitals logging.
2. **Doctor Consultation**: Clinical examination and diagnosis.
3. **Diagnostics / Lab Testing**: Blood tests, imaging, or specialized scans.
4. **Treatment / Procedure**: Medication, therapy, or surgical intervention.
5. **Discharge or Admission**: Final exit or transfer to inpatient ward.
